# Owl Bakery customer policies

This document is fictional demo content for Owl Bakery. It is the approved source for
general company policies. Customer-specific order details must come from the order system or
customer memory, not from this document.

## Delivery

- Standard delivery is available within 8 miles of the bakery and costs £6.
- Orders of £60 or more receive free standard delivery.
- Customers choose a two-hour delivery window. Exact arrival times are not guaranteed.
- Birthday and event orders can include a requested deadline. The bakery aims to arrive at
  least 30 minutes before that deadline, but the current ETA must be checked in the live order
  system.
- A recipient aged 18 or over must be available. Drivers wait for up to 10 minutes and attempt
  one phone call before returning the order to the bakery.
- Cakes cannot be left outside or with a neighbour unless the customer explicitly authorises it.
- Delivery addresses may be changed until 10:00 AM on the delivery day. Changes outside the
  normal delivery area may incur a fee or require pickup.

## Pickup

- Pickup is available Tuesday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM.
- Customers should provide the order number and the name on the order.
- Orders are held until closing time on the scheduled pickup day. Refrigerated orders may be
  held until noon the following business day, after which quality cannot be guaranteed.
- A nominated person may collect an order if they know the order number and collection name.
- Switching from pickup to delivery is subject to delivery capacity and must be confirmed in
  the live order system.

## Allergens and dietary requests

- The bakery handles wheat, gluten, eggs, milk, soya, peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame in the same
  kitchen.
- Staff can provide ingredient and allergen information for each standard product.
- Products described as made without gluten, dairy, nuts, or animal ingredients are prepared
  with suitable recipes, but the shared kitchen cannot guarantee they are free from traces.
- The bakery does not claim that any product is safe for a severe allergy or coeliac disease.
- Customers with allergies must speak with bakery staff before ordering. The agent must not
  infer allergen safety from a product name or from a previous purchase.
- Chocolate celebration cake contains wheat, eggs, milk, and soya. It may contain traces of
  peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame.

## Changes, cancellations, and refunds

- Flavour, inscription, fulfilment method, and address changes are accepted when production and
  delivery capacity allow.
- Standard cakes can be cancelled for a full refund at least 48 hours before fulfilment.
- Custom cakes require seven days' cancellation notice for a full refund. Design deposits become
  non-refundable once production begins.
- Approved refunds return to the original payment method within five to ten business days.
- An agent must confirm that an update succeeded in the order system before telling the customer
  that their order changed.

## Custom cakes

- Simple celebration cakes require at least 72 hours' notice.
- Sculpted, tiered, wedding, or hand-painted cakes require at least 14 days' notice.
- Final servings, flavours, inscription, design, fulfilment method, and event deadline must be
  confirmed before production begins.
- Colour matching is approximate because icing colours vary between batches and lighting.

## Cake care and storage

- Keep buttercream and fresh-cream cakes refrigerated until 60 minutes before serving.
- Keep fondant cakes in a cool, dry room and do not refrigerate unless staff instruct otherwise.
- Transport cakes on a flat vehicle floor, not on a seat or a passenger's lap.
- For best quality, serve cakes on the fulfilment day. Refrigerated leftovers should be consumed
  within two days.

## Damaged, late, or incorrect orders

- Customers should report damage or an incorrect item within two hours of delivery or pickup and
  provide photographs when possible.
- The bakery may offer a replacement, partial refund, or full refund after reviewing the issue.
- Current driver location and ETA must always come from the live order system, never from memory
  or a previous conversation.

## Privacy and identity verification

- Staff may discuss an order with a caller only after matching the caller to the order or
  verifying the order number and one additional detail.
- Addresses, birthdays, contact details, and previous conversations must never be disclosed to a
  different customer.
- A customer calling from an unknown number must be verified before that number is linked to an
  existing profile.
